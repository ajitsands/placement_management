3. Operational Challenges & Milestone Automation Need

An architectural review of the client's current operational spreadsheet workflows (Placement and Claims Sheet 2026) identifies critical operational risks inherent to managing over 480 candidate placements and hundreds of claims in manual files. The proposed web system directly addresses these vulnerabilities by establishing automated milestone monitoring, strict role-based governance, and immutable financial audit trails:

3.1 Missed Claim Milestones & Financial Leakage

Vulnerability: Employment claims span a 12-month lifecycle (Initial Placement, 6 Months, and 12 Months). In Excel, identifying due claims requires staff to manually scan dates. Milestones reaching +180 or +365 days are frequently delayed or missed, causing blocked cashflow and claim leakage.
Solution: Automated daily cron engine monitors employment start dates and dispatches proactive dashboard badge alerts and email reminders to responsible case managers before milestones lapse.

3.2 Lack of Multi-User Concurrency & Access Control

Vulnerability: Multiple case managers (Husain, Dina, Meaad, Malak, Zainab Mattar, etc.) currently access a single shared spreadsheet. This creates severe file-locking conflicts, risk of unauthorized edits to salary or claims, and accidental cell corruption.
Solution: Enterprise Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Each case manager has an authenticated individual login with access restricted to assigned candidates, while Administrators maintain global oversight.

3.3 Unstructured Dispute Handling & Audit Non-Compliance

Vulnerability: Disputed claims (resignation, probation termination, employer disagreement) are recorded as unstructured text notes. If a dispute fails, records are often deleted from active sheets, destroying historical audit trails required for regulatory inspection.
Solution: Governed dispute state machine. Captures structured dispute categories and remarks, enables safe reinstatement to active collection, and ensures non-destructive permanent archival for terminated claims.

3.4 Decoupled Collections & Financial Reconciliation Gaps

Vulnerability: Payment receipts are not systematically linked to individual claims. Tracking partial payments, cheques, and remaining candidate balances across multiple sheets leads to financial blind spots and manual reconciliation errors.
Solution: Dedicated claims payment ledger supporting multi-receipt partial collections, automatic remaining balance calculation, receipt logging, and 1-click reconciliation reports.

The proposed solution digitizes the full operational lifecycle into four interconnected workflow pipelines: Candidate Onboarding, Milestone Automation, Dispute Resolution, and Financial Ledger Reconciliation.

4.2 Claim Status & Dispute State Machine

Every claim moves through a deterministic finite-state machine ensuring zero ambiguity between financial collection and legal or employer dispute states:

A. Undisputed Workflow

Claims verified and accepted for active collection. Instantly dispatched to the payment collection module to record partial receipts, track bank reference numbers, and reconcile outstanding balances.

B. Disputed Handling

Authorized case managers record dispute reasons (e.g., candidate resignation, termination during probation, contract discrepancy). Once resolved, claims can be safely reinstated to undisputed status without data loss.

C. Non-Destructive Termination Workflow

If a claim cannot be recovered, it is marked as Terminated with documented reason and manager approval. In strict compliance with regulatory accounting standards, terminated claims are removed from active accounts receivable but remain 100% permanently archived for future audit and review.

5. Scope of Work: Detailed Functional Modules

The proposed software system encompasses seven core functional modules, each engineered to address specific operational and regulatory needs:

Module 1: User Management & Access Control

  • Administrator Role: Full system governance, creation of case manager accounts, master setting configuration, and audit logs.
  • Case Manager Role: Individual logins (Husain, Dina, Meaad, Malak, Zainab Mattar, etc.) with scoped access to manage their assigned candidate portfolios.
  • Audit Trail: System tracks timestamp and user identity for every modification or claim status update.

Module 2: Candidate Registration & Tracking

  • Demographics & Verification: CPR validation, full legal name, gender, education level, and contact info.
  • Employment Parameters: Employer CR number, company name, position, salary, and employment type.
  • Automatic Reference Generation: Unique identifier formatting (e.g., JCP/26/268524) with duplicate prevention.

Module 3: Milestone Automation & Alerts

  • Trigger 1 (Placement): Immediate eligibility upon confirmation of joining date.
  • Trigger 2 (6 Months): Automated trigger at Joining Date + 180 Days.
  • Trigger 3 (12 Months): Automated trigger at Joining Date + 365 Days.
  • Proactive Alerts: Dashboard badge counters and automated email alerts sent to the assigned case manager.

Module 4: Manual Claim Amount Entry & Milestone Recording

  • Manual Amount Input: Authorized case managers directly input the agreed claim amount in BHD for each candidate milestone upon generation. The system does not calculate amounts automatically.
  • Milestone Binding & Metadata: System captures claim date, milestone category (Placement, 6M, 12M), candidate reference number, entered amount, and case manager identity.
  • Duplicate Claim Prevention: Prohibits duplicate claim entries for the same candidate and milestone while maintaining complete historical status logs.

Module 5: Claims Dispute & Termination

  • Categorized Dispute Filing: Classifies disputes (probation termination, employer dispute, wage issue).
  • Resolution Workflow: Reinstates validated claims back to active collection or transitions unrecoverable claims to Terminated status.
  • Immutable History: Zero physical deletion of historical claims.

Module 6: Payment Collection & Ledger

  • Multi-Receipt Collections: Allows recording multiple partial payments against a single claim.
  • Financial Reconciliation: Automatically computes Collected Amount and Outstanding Balance.
  • Audit Reference: Logs cheque number, bank transfer reference, receipt date, and cashier notes.

Module 7: Executive Dashboards & 10 Core Management Reports

Comprehensive reporting suite supporting date ranges, case manager portfolio filters, and 1-click export to Excel / PDF:

• Candidate Placement Report • Terminated Claims Report • Claim Milestones Report (Day 0, 180, 365) • Payment Collection Report • Unclaimed Milestones Report • Outstanding Balance Report • Claims Summary Report • Financial Reconciliation Report • Disputed Claims Report • Case Manager KPI Performance Report

Multi-Status Dispute & Financial Settlement Lifecycle

1. Claimable: The candidate reaches eligibility criteria; system flags claim on case manager dashboard.
2. Claimed: Case manager reviews details, adjusts any authorized deductions, and generates the claim invoice.
3. Disputed: If an employer terminates or disputes candidate attendance, claim is marked as Disputed with full audit remarks.
4. Reinstated: Once resolved with the employer or Ministry, claim returns safely to Undisputed status without loss of prior records.
5. Terminated: If unrecoverable, claim is marked Terminated (permanently preserved for financial audit, removed from active receivables).
6. Partial & Settled Payments: Tracks multi-receipt partial payments, cheques, and transfers until remaining balance reaches BHD 0.000.
